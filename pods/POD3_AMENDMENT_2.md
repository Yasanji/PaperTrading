# Pod 3: Amendment 2

Dated 8 October 2026, before any prediction for the December 2026 STOXX Europe 600 review is committed. It sets how STOXX predictions are made while STOXX's own selection lists are not available. The trade, sizing, costs and pass marks are unchanged.

## 1. Why

The pre-registration says STOXX predictions use STOXX's monthly selection lists. Those lists could not be downloaded on 8 October 2026. Until they can, the ranking below is used. If a selection list for the rank date is obtained before the commitment date, it replaces the ranking, and the change is recorded with the prediction.

## 2. Rules applied

1. **Universe.** Current STOXX Europe 600 members (Xtrackers STOXX Europe 600 fund holdings) plus MSCI Europe and MSCI Europe Small Cap constituents that are not members. Other share classes of members and companies with only a US listing are left out.
2. **Ranking.** By free-float market value in USD. Members use STOXX fund weights; non-members use MSCI fund weights. Both are scaled to USD with Yahoo market values.
3. **Selection.** Every stock ranked 1 to 550 is selected. The remaining places go to current members ranked 551 to 750 in rank order, then to the highest-ranked non-members. The number of places equals the current number of fund lines.
4. **Calls.** Selected non-members are predicted additions; members not selected are predicted deletions. Non-members ranked 551 to 600 and selected members ranked below 560 are listed as watch names.
5. **Rank date.** The last trading day of November, 30 November 2026. The prediction is committed by 19 November, 20 trading days before the effective date, using the latest prices then available.

## 3. Check on the September 2026 review

Membership before the review was rebuilt from current holdings and STOXX's announcement of 1 September 2026, and stocks were ranked at 31 August prices.

| | Predicted | Right | Actual |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Additions | 13 | 12 | 14 |
| Deletions | 14 | 9 | 14 |

Nine of the fourteen additions were Greek stocks, added when Greece was reclassified as a developed market. Members' free-float values come from fund weights after the review, which favours the check. Three actual deletions were not in the data.

## 4. Limits

Not modelled: STOXX's own free-float and liquidity screens, Polish and Greek non-members not held by MSCI funds, corporate events, and fast entry or exit between reviews.
