# Paper 2 and the paper book

Fixed on 5 October 2026, alongside the pre-registration. This rule is part of the paper book's process and not part of the paper.

The local rebalancing overlay enters the paper book only if the local signal passes in the main sample and is positive after costs in the hold-out. It is then sized to a fixed share of the book's risk, within the 4% drawdown limit, and its forward results are reported from the date it is added.
