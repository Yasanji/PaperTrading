Glossary

Volatility surface

Implied vol for every strike (moneyness) and expiry — the full 3-D surface. Skew: puts (left) richer than calls; term structure: vol vs expiry.

Term structure

ATM vol by tenor today (9d→6m); slope VIX3M−VIX vs history. Positive = contango (calm); negative = backwardation (stress).

Skew

Live 25-delta risk reversal (put − call vol) and butterfly by expiry; CBOE SKEW index vs history.

Signals

Where each metric sits in its 1- and 5-year range — the relative-value read.
